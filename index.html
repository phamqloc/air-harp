Air Harp

Draw strings of light, then play them.

  • Touch: drag to stretch a string. Swipe across strings to pluck them.
  • Camera: pinch your thumb and index finger, pull, and release to stretch a string. Sweep a fingertip through it to play.
  • Shorter strings play higher notes. Every note fits the same pentatonic scale, so anything you play sounds good.

You'll hear a short chime when sound is on. If it's quiet, turn up the volume or switch off Silent Mode.
